## Quick Overview

* **Problem Type:** Array, Two Pointers, Greedy
* **Difficulty:** Medium
* **Key Concept:** Two Pointers
* **Solution:** Use two pointers at both ends of the array. Calculate the water area between them and move the pointer with the smaller height inward, because the shorter side limits the maximum area.
