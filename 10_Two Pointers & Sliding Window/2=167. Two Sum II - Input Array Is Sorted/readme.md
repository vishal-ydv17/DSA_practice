## Quick Overview

* **Problem Type:** Array, Two Pointers
* **Difficulty:** Medium
* **Key Concept:** Two Pointers
* **Solution:** Use two pointers at the beginning and end of the sorted array. If their sum is too small, move the left pointer right; if it is too large, move the right pointer left until the target sum is found.
