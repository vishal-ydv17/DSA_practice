## Quick Overview

* **Problem Type:** String, Stack
* **Difficulty:** Easy
* **Key Concept:** Parentheses Balance & Traversal
* **Solution:** Maintain a balance counter while traversing the string. Increase it for `(` and decrease it for `)`. Skip the outermost parentheses of every primitive substring by adding a character only when the balance is inside the primitive.
