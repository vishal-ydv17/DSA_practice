## Quick Overview

* **Problem Type**: Greedy, Array

* **Difficulty**: Medium

* **Key Concept**: Greedy & Running Balance

* **Solution**: Calculate the total gas and total cost first. If the total gas is less than the total cost, completing the circuit is impossible. Otherwise, keep a running balance while traversing the stations. If the balance becomes negative, the current starting point cannot work, so reset the starting station to the next index and reset the balance to `0`.
