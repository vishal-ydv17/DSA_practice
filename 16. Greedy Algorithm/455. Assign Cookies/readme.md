## Quick Overview

* **Problem Type**: Greedy, Sorting, Two Pointers, Array

* **Difficulty**: Easy

* **Key Concept**: Greedy & Two Pointers

* **Solution**: Sort both the children's greed factors and the cookie sizes. Use two pointers to match each child with the smallest cookie that can satisfy their greed. If a cookie is large enough, assign it to the child and move both pointers; otherwise, move to the next larger cookie.
