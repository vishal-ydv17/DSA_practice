## Quick Overview

* **Problem Type**: Greedy, Sorting, Array

* **Difficulty**: Medium

* **Key Concept**: Greedy & Sorting

* **Solution**: Calculate the cost difference between sending each person to City A and City B. Sort people based on this difference and send the first half to City A and the remaining half to City B. This greedy approach minimizes the total cost while ensuring exactly `n` people are sent to each city.
